Privacy Policy

Comment-Automation ("we", "our", or "the app") is an experimental Instagram automation project. This policy describes information the app may process when a developer or authorized tester connects an Instagram professional account.

Information we process

Depending on enabled features, we may receive Instagram account identifiers, usernames, post and comment identifiers, comment text, message-related identifiers, and webhook event metadata provided by Meta's APIs. Authentication credentials and access tokens are used to connect authorized accounts.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate connected accounts, detect relevant comment events, test automated replies, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain the app's security and reliability. We do not sell Instagram data.

Storage and retention

During development, webhook payloads and technical errors may appear in server or hosting logs. Access tokens may be stored in the app's private configuration. We aim to limit access and retain data only as needed for testing, debugging, security, or applicable legal obligations. Hosting providers may apply their own log-retention schedules.

Sharing and third parties

The app interacts with Meta's Instagram APIs and may use hosting infrastructure to run its backend and store logs. These providers process information under their own applicable terms and policies. We do not disclose Instagram data to advertisers.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS connections and restricted access to credentials. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Authorized testers can stop using the app and revoke its access through the applicable Meta or Instagram account settings. You may request deletion of information under our control using the instructions below.
