03 / YOUR DATA

Data Deletion Instructions

If you have used or tested Comment-Automation and would like information associated with you or your Instagram account removed from our systems, you can submit a deletion request.

  1. Email choudharyanuj268@gmail.com with the subject Comment-Automation Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the Instagram username or account identifier associated with your request. Do not send your password or access token.
  3. We may ask for reasonable verification to ensure the request relates to your account.
  4. We will review the request, delete applicable data under our control where feasible, and reply with the outcome. Some limited information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

You can also revoke the app's access in your Instagram/Meta account settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but may not automatically erase existing diagnostic logs; please email us for deletion.
